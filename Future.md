# The Engine's Future (O futuro da Engine)

## ENGLISH

Hi everyone, I’m here to talk about the future of the Cvncfs Engine...

The engine stopped receiving updates a while ago, so I felt I had to do something, since this project is important to me.

### Problems
 
* Time

I have little free time because I attend a military school, so I built this engine as a sort of hobby. I was also testing my HTML and JS skills.

* My laziness

Besides, assembling this engine seems a bit labor-intensive, but the results... are good, and I love it.
The results are the "why" behind my creating this engine. And that is also the reason I’m coming back to it (I had **actually retired it**, calling it "Legacy").
But now I'll talk about future ideas.

### Future

There's **A LOT** planned for the future, assuming Funkin' Crew doesn't add it on their own. Right now, I see this engine as an "add-on" to V-Slice, but at the same time... I feel like it could be something better than just — let's say — **a simple thing**.

Some ideas...

1. New UI and new scripts
1. New editors (Character Editor - Week; Week Editor updated and others...)
1. Support for other languages ​​(English, Spanish and Portuguese) and dark theme.
1. Simple loading for mods developed here (to check for structural errors)
